## Journey 旅程

Started tinkering with computers as a kid—exploring systems and getting lost in games. Studied business in undergrad, then pivoted to CS for my master's. Never became a "pure" engineer, but found my way through building: crawlers, NLP pipelines, sentiment analysis systems, and data infrastructure that processes millions of records.

The path from business to code taught me something: the best systems aren't just technically sound—they're shaped by understanding people and problems first.
